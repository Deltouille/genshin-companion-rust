Genshin Companion 0.6.0 lets you choose which account a HoYoLAB sync goes to, puts your characters, weapons, plan and inventory in the snapshot export, and gives the planner a "?" material for each type you don't know yet.

## Download

| System | Archive |
|---|---|
| Windows (x86_64) | `genshin-companion-0.6.0-windows-x86_64.zip` |
| macOS 11 or later, Apple Silicon | `genshin-companion-0.6.0-macos-apple-silicon.zip` |
| macOS 11 or later, Intel | `genshin-companion-0.6.0-macos-intel.zip` |
| Linux (x86_64, glibc 2.35 or later) | `genshin-companion-0.6.0-linux-x86_64.tar.gz` |

Unpack the archive and start the app: there is nothing to install. Your data stays where it was, so a newer version picks up your history, plan and settings. `SHA256SUMS.txt` holds the archives' checksums.

The builds aren't signed by a developer account, so the first launch asks for confirmation:

- **Windows:** if SmartScreen warns, choose *More info*, then *Run anyway*.
- **macOS:** macOS refuses to open it the first time. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security and choose *Open Anyway*.
- **Linux:** the app needs fontconfig, freetype, libxkbcommon, Wayland, libX11, libxcb and the Vulkan loader, which desktop systems usually have.

## What's new

**The HoYoLAB sync goes to the account you choose.** It offers the same accounts as the import from the game: the one that already has your UID, otherwise a new account or one of yours without a UID (the one shown is suggested when it has none). The preview follows your choice, and the account gets your UID, so the next import from the game lands in it too. Before, a sync into an account without a UID made a second account, and the next import from the game went there.

**Weapon swaps.** When two characters swapped weapons in the game, the sync now moves your copies between them (shown as ⇄ in the preview) instead of adding two new ones.

**The snapshot carries your whole account.** The snapshot export and the mirror folder now hold each account's characters, weapons, plan and inventory beside its wishes, and the mirror is updated after every change to them. Importing a snapshot never lowers anything:

- a character or weapon copy is added, or raised to the higher level, constellation, talents or refinement;
- a plan entry is added only when that character, weapon or custom name isn't planned yet;
- a material is counted only when you have none of it.

So importing the same snapshot twice changes nothing the second time.

**Planner:**

- Each type of material (talent book, boss material, gem, local specialty, weekly boss material, weapon material, enemy drops) has a "?" for a custom character or weapon whose material isn't known yet. It comes first when you choose a material, and it is counted, crafted and farmed like the real one: a character to 90 with every talent to 10 needs 9, 63 and 114 "?" talent books, and the resin shows their domain runs. Once the real material is known, edit the entry and pick it.
- In the inventory, *You have* is left empty when you have none, so you type a count without erasing a 0 first.

**Safety and privacy:**

- The HoYoLAB dialog and the README warn that the cookie values give access to your HoYoLAB account, and that a clipboard history may keep them.
- If the app can't start or crashes, `genshin-companion-errors.txt` now goes to your cache folder (`~/.cache/genshin-companion/` on Linux, `~/Library/Caches/genshin-companion/` on macOS, `%LOCALAPPDATA%\genshin-companion\cache\` on Windows), readable by you alone, and starts over past 256 KiB.
- A `settings.json` that can't be read is set aside with the time in its name, so one set aside earlier is never overwritten.
- The Credits page shows genshin.py's licence, which the HoYoLAB sync follows.

## Good to know

- A snapshot exported by 0.6.0 is version 2: 0.5.0 and earlier refuse it as too new. Snapshots from earlier versions still import.
- Undoing a snapshot import removes its wishes. To also undo what it added to your characters, weapons, plan and inventory, restore the backup taken before the import (Settings → Backups).
- If an earlier HoYoLAB sync already made a second account for your UID, the two can't be merged yet.
- The app contains none of the game's images. It downloads each one from paimon.moe or Project Amber the first time a page shows it, then keeps it. To use the app offline, Settings → Images downloads them all at once.
