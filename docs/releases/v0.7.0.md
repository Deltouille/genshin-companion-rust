Genshin Companion 0.7.0 speaks French: the whole app, and the game's own names and texts. It also shows what a HoYoLAB sync couldn't read, and keeps up better when you change things quickly.

## Download

| System | Archive |
|---|---|
| Windows (x86_64) | `genshin-companion-0.7.0-windows-x86_64.zip` |
| macOS 11 or later, Apple Silicon | `genshin-companion-0.7.0-macos-apple-silicon.zip` |
| macOS 11 or later, Intel | `genshin-companion-0.7.0-macos-intel.zip` |
| Linux (x86_64, glibc 2.35 or later) | `genshin-companion-0.7.0-linux-x86_64.tar.gz` |

Unpack the archive and start the app: there is nothing to install. Your data stays where it was, so a newer version picks up your history, plan and settings. `SHA256SUMS.txt` holds the archives' checksums.

The builds aren't signed by a developer account, so the first launch asks for confirmation:

- **Windows:** if SmartScreen warns, choose *More info*, then *Run anyway*.
- **macOS:** macOS refuses to open it the first time. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security and choose *Open Anyway*.
- **Linux:** the app needs fontconfig, freetype, libxkbcommon, Wayland, libX11, libxcb and the Vulkan loader, which desktop systems usually have.

## What's new

**English and French.** Every page, dialog and message is in both languages, and so are the game's own texts: the names of characters, weapons and materials, their descriptions, talents with their values ("DGT 1er coup : 31,7 %"), constellations and weapon passives, and the items of your wish history, as the game writes them in French (from genshin-db). Numbers and dates follow the language: "12 800", "1,6 %", "9 nov. 2026 à 12:20".

The app opens in your system's language when it has it, else in English. *Settings → Language* changes it at once, without restarting.

**The HoYoLAB sync says what it couldn't read.** Its preview now lists what the sync left unclear or out, before you confirm: a character at a level cap whose ascension HoYoLAB doesn't tell (counted as not ascended: set it on their page), a level above 90, a character or weapon this version doesn't know yet, talents that couldn't be read.

**Quicker and steadier:**

- Two quick changes in a row (dragging a planner slider, clicking +1 twice) are now always saved in the order you made them; before, the older one could win.
- After a change, only the account it touches is worked out again, and an image arriving no longer rebuilds the pages: the app keeps up better on large histories.
- The mirror folder's copy is written a few seconds after your last change, and when the app closes, rather than after every single click.
- A failure that happens in the background (a backup, a restore, an export, remembering the game folder) is always reported, even if you started something else meanwhile.
- Error messages say what you can do ("update the app, then import the file again"), never a developer's command.

## Good to know

- Banner names stay in English: paimon.moe, where they come from, has them in English only.
- Your settings and exports keep their formats: 0.6.0 still reads a snapshot exported by 0.7.0, and ignores the language setting.
- The accounts an import creates are still named "Main" or "Account 3" in either language, since the next import finds the account by that name.
- The app contains none of the game's images. It downloads each one from paimon.moe or Project Amber the first time a page shows it, then keeps it. To use the app offline, Settings → Images downloads them all at once.
