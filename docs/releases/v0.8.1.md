Genshin Companion 0.8.1 tells you what to farm today, and lets you add, edit and remove accounts by hand. It also brings everything 0.8.0 was meant to: 0.8.0 was never published, as one of its builds couldn't run. So, from 0.7.0: import your whole bag from a GOOD file, browse every material in Archive → Resources, and find the archive's lists on one card.

## Download

| System | Archive |
|---|---|
| Windows (x86_64) | `genshin-companion-0.8.1-windows-x86_64.zip` |
| macOS 11 or later, Apple Silicon | `genshin-companion-0.8.1-macos-apple-silicon.zip` |
| macOS 11 or later, Intel | `genshin-companion-0.8.1-macos-intel.zip` |
| Linux (x86_64, glibc 2.35 or later) | `genshin-companion-0.8.1-linux-x86_64.tar.gz` |

Unpack the archive and start the app: there is nothing to install. Your data stays where it was, so a newer version picks up your history, plan and settings. `SHA256SUMS.txt` holds the archives' checksums.

The builds aren't signed by a developer account, so the first launch asks for confirmation:

- **Windows:** if SmartScreen warns, choose *More info*, then *Run anyway*.
- **macOS:** macOS refuses to open it the first time. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security and choose *Open Anyway*.
- **Linux:** the app needs fontconfig, freetype, libxkbcommon, Wayland, libX11, libxcb and the Vulkan loader, which desktop systems usually have.

## What's new

**Farm today.** The planner opens on the domains open today that give what your plan still lacks once your bag is shared out: talent books and weapon ascension materials, the tiers still missing, and the portraits of who needs them. The day is the game's: it starts at 4:00 in your account's server time, and on Sunday every domain is open. When no domain gives what the plan lacks today, the card names the next day one does. It follows the day on its own, even with the app left open overnight.

**Accounts by hand.** Settings → Accounts lists your accounts with their UID, server, wishes and characters:

- *Add…* makes one: a name, and optionally its UID and server, for a player who imports no wishes yet, or another account of yours;
- *Edit…* renames one, and gives an account without a UID its UID or only its server; the server follows a UID typed. Once given, a UID stays, since the imports find the account by it;
- *Remove…* removes an account that has no wishes, with its characters, weapons, plan and inventory, after asking. A backup is taken first, so *Restore…* brings it back.

An account's server sets the wish banners' times and the planner's day: give one to an account a GOOD import made, which has none.

**Import a GOOD file.** GOOD is the format [Irminsul](https://github.com/konkers/irminsul), [Inventory Kamera](https://github.com/Andrewthe13th/Inventory_Kamera) and [Genshin Optimizer](https://frzyc.github.io/genshin-optimizer/) export. *Import → Import a GOOD file…* (also in the app's menu) reads one from a file, or from the clipboard, where Irminsul can copy its export, into the account shown:

- your characters, with their level, ascension, constellations and talents;
- every weapon in your bag, with its level, refinement and who holds it;
- your materials, which the planner then shares out: no more typing them one by one.

A preview shows every change before anything is written, the hundred identical fodder weapons on one line ("Cool Steel · 1/20 · R1 ×106"), and says what it leaves out. Characters and weapons are only added or raised, never lowered; the materials take the file's counts. A backup comes first, and importing the same file twice changes nothing.

**Archive → Resources.** Every material of the game, sorted as the game's bag sorts them: Mora and EXP, ascension gems, boss and weekly boss materials, talent materials, weapon ascension materials, common and elite enemy drops, and each region's local specialties. Each card shows how many you have; search by name, or show only what you own. Click one to set its count, with the rest of its series beside it.

**The archive's lists match.** Characters, weapons and resources share one card, the same size on every page.

**A weapon's page counts your copies**, and shows copies alike once ("×5"), so a bag full of fodder stays readable. *Remove one* takes one copy of the row.

**Smaller fixes:**

- The custom character and weapon dialog keeps its materials level, with their names on two lines, in every language; in French, "Matériau hebdomadaire" ran onto two lines and pushed its tile down.
- The planner's inventory dialog wraps the line under a material's name instead of running out of the dialog, as it did in French.
- The sidebar's pages lose the small diamond beside their name.

## Good to know

- Without a UID or a server, "Farm today" follows your computer's clock and says so: give the account its UID or server in Settings → Accounts.
- An account with wishes can't be removed: its history is the one thing the app never loses.
- Irminsul's exports carry no Mora: the import keeps the Mora you entered. A material the planner uses that the file lacks goes to 0, since the file describes your whole bag.
- Food, gadgets and quest items aren't in the game data the app carries, so the import leaves them out (the preview counts them), and Resources doesn't list them.
- The Manekins are still left out: their talents aren't in the game data yet.
- The app only reads the GOOD file. How the tool that wrote it reads your game is between you and that tool: Irminsul reads the game's network traffic, Inventory Kamera reads your screen.
- Your data and exports keep their formats: nothing in the database changes, and 0.7.0 still reads a snapshot exported by 0.8.1.
- The app contains none of the game's images. It downloads each one from paimon.moe or Project Amber the first time a page shows it, then keeps it. To use the app offline, Settings → Images downloads them all at once.
