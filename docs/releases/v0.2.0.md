Genshin Companion 0.2.0 opens on an overview of your account, keeps track of the characters you own, and takes on the look of the game's menus.

## Download

| System | Archive |
|---|---|
| Windows (x86_64) | `genshin-companion-0.2.0-windows-x86_64.zip` |
| macOS 11 or later, Apple Silicon | `genshin-companion-0.2.0-macos-apple-silicon.zip` |
| macOS 11 or later, Intel | `genshin-companion-0.2.0-macos-intel.zip` |
| Linux (x86_64, glibc 2.35 or later) | `genshin-companion-0.2.0-linux-x86_64.tar.gz` |

Unpack the archive and start the app: there is nothing to install. Your data stays where it was, so a newer version picks up your history, plan and settings. `SHA256SUMS.txt` holds the archives' checksums.

The builds aren't signed by a developer account, so the first launch asks for confirmation:

- **Windows:** if SmartScreen warns, choose *More info*, then *Run anyway*.
- **macOS:** macOS refuses to open it the first time. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security and choose *Open Anyway*.
- **Linux:** the app needs fontconfig, freetype, libxkbcommon, Wayland, libX11, libxcb and the Vulkan loader, which desktop systems usually have.

## What's new

**Overview.** The app opens on your account in figures, one tab each:

- **Wishes:** the former overview: key figures, a card per banner type, wishes per month and the pity of each 5★;
- **Characters:** how many of the game's characters you own, by rarity, element and weapon, then your constellations, your characters at level 90 and your talents at level 10;
- **Weapons:** the weapons your wishes brought, by rarity and type, then each 4★ and 5★ with the refinement its copies make.

The Wishes group in the sidebar now lists the banner types only.

**Your characters.** Archive → Characters lists the characters you own first, with their level and constellation, then the others, faded. Add one with +, remove it with × (it asks first), and mark your favourites with ☆ (a filter shows them alone). A character's page has a *My character* panel for its level, constellation and talents, and its sliders and *Add to the planner* start from them.

**One plan per account.** The plan, the inventory and your characters belong to the account picked in the sidebar. On the first start, the app backs up the database, then gives an existing plan and inventory to your oldest account (or to a new "Main" account when there is none).

**A new look**, after Genshin Impact's menus, in colours and shapes only (none of the game's art): an ink-blue slate with parchment text in the dark theme, a parchment page with an ink sidebar in the light one, every item on its rarity tile over a parchment strip, and rounded controls throughout.

- The character and weapon lists have a search, a *Filter* drawer, a *Sort by* menu and a button that reverses the order.
- A character's page is one page of sections: My character, Stats, Ascension, Talents, Constellations.
- Menus are parchment lists you can search and use with the keyboard.

## Good to know

- The Weapons tab counts the weapons from your wishes only: copies from the shop, events and the forge aren't wishes, so the game may show a higher refinement.
- Your characters, plan and inventory are in the database and its backups, but not yet in the snapshot export or the mirror folder.
- The app contains none of the game's images. It downloads each one from paimon.moe or Project Amber the first time a page shows it, then keeps it. To use the app offline, Settings → Images downloads them all at once.
