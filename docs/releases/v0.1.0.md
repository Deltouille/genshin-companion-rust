The first release of Genshin Companion. It is a desktop companion for Genshin Impact: a wish counter that never loses your history, a character and weapon archive, and an ascension planner. Your data stays on your computer.

## Download

| System | Archive |
|---|---|
| Windows (x86_64) | `genshin-companion-0.1.0-windows-x86_64.zip` |
| macOS 11 or later, Apple Silicon | `genshin-companion-0.1.0-macos-apple-silicon.zip` |
| macOS 11 or later, Intel | `genshin-companion-0.1.0-macos-intel.zip` |
| Linux (x86_64, glibc 2.35 or later) | `genshin-companion-0.1.0-linux-x86_64.tar.gz` |

Unpack the archive and start the app: there is nothing to install. `SHA256SUMS.txt` holds the archives' checksums.

The builds aren't signed by a developer account, so the first launch asks for confirmation:

- **Windows:** if SmartScreen warns, choose *More info*, then *Run anyway*.
- **macOS:** macOS refuses to open it the first time. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security and choose *Open Anyway*.
- **Linux:** the app needs fontconfig, freetype, libxkbcommon, Wayland, libX11, libxcb and the Vulkan loader, which desktop systems usually have.

## What it does

**Wish counter**, a remake of [paimon.moe](https://paimon.moe)'s, with the same numbers:

- pity, 50/50 and guarantees for every banner type (the Character Event banners share their pity);
- an overview with key figures and a card per banner type: the pity and the odds of the next 5★ and 4★, the 50/50 record and every 5★ with its pity, then wishes per month and the pity of each 5★;
- the history as a table you can sort, or banner by banner;
- counting by hand (+1, +10, one 4★ or 5★), turned on in Settings.

**Imports and exports:**

- from the game: the app finds the wish history link in the game's cache (Windows, or a Wine/Proton prefix on Linux), or you paste it from any device;
- from a paimon.moe save or spreadsheet, a [UIGF](https://uigf.org) v4 file, or the app's own snapshot;
- to the snapshot, UIGF v4 and paimon.moe's spreadsheet.

**Your history is never lost:** imports only add wishes, a backup of the database is taken before each one, and every import can be undone. A mirror folder (optional) keeps a copy of your history in a folder you sync yourself.

**Archive:** every character (stats at any level, talents with their values, constellations, ascension cost) and every weapon (stats at any level, passive at each refinement).

**Ascension planner**, like [genshin-center](https://genshin-center.com/planner)'s:

- characters and weapons, from their current level and talents to the ones you want, with the materials per card and for the whole plan;
- your inventory, shared out over the plan in its order (drag the cards to change it), with lower tiers crafted into higher ones;
- custom characters and weapons for those the game data doesn't have yet.

## Good to know

- The app contains none of the game's images. It downloads each one from paimon.moe or Project Amber the first time a page shows it, then keeps it. To use the app offline, Settings → Images downloads them all at once.
- The planner's plan and inventory are in the database and its backups, but not yet in the snapshot export or the mirror folder.
