Genshin Companion 0.3.0 brings your characters in from HoYoLAB, gathers every import on one page, and makes the ascension planner easier to read.

## Download

| System | Archive |
|---|---|
| Windows (x86_64) | `genshin-companion-0.3.0-windows-x86_64.zip` |
| macOS 11 or later, Apple Silicon | `genshin-companion-0.3.0-macos-apple-silicon.zip` |
| macOS 11 or later, Intel | `genshin-companion-0.3.0-macos-intel.zip` |
| Linux (x86_64, glibc 2.35 or later) | `genshin-companion-0.3.0-linux-x86_64.tar.gz` |

Unpack the archive and start the app: there is nothing to install. Your data stays where it was, so a newer version picks up your history, plan and settings. `SHA256SUMS.txt` holds the archives' checksums.

The builds aren't signed by a developer account, so the first launch asks for confirmation:

- **Windows:** if SmartScreen warns, choose *More info*, then *Run anyway*.
- **macOS:** macOS refuses to open it the first time. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security and choose *Open Anyway*.
- **Linux:** the app needs fontconfig, freetype, libxkbcommon, Wayland, libX11, libxcb and the Vulkan loader, which desktop systems usually have.

## What's new

**Your characters from HoYoLAB.** *Sync from HoYoLAB* reads your characters as they are in the game (level, constellations, talents) and the weapon each holds, and adds them to your collection. It shows what would change first, and only adds or raises what you entered, never lowers it. It needs your HoYoLAB cookie: in your browser, open hoyolab.com, then the developer tools (F12) → Storage → Cookies, and copy `ltuid_v2`, `ltoken_v2` and `ltmid_v2`. The app uses it for that sync only: it never saves it, and sends it to HoYoverse only.

**An Import page.** Every way to bring data in is on one page: the wish history (from the game, or from a file) and the HoYoLAB sync. The buttons also stay on the pages that had them.

**The ascension planner:**

- *Add a character* and *Add a weapon* open a grid of cards with a search, yours first. A character you own starts from your levels and talents.
- EXP shows as Hero's Wit alone (Mystic Enhancement Ore for weapons); clicking it shows the three books (or ores), each counted for its EXP.
- A flask badge on a material says how many are crafted from the tiers below, as on genshin-center.
- The inventory dialog shows, for each tier, what the plan needs, what it crafts and what is still needed, and updates as you type.

## Good to know

- HoYoLAB doesn't show the weapons in your bag or your materials, nor whether a character at a level cap (20, 40 … 80) ascended: such a character counts as not yet ascended, and levels above 90 count as 90/90. Raise them on the character's page if needed.
- HoYoLAB has no public API: the sync follows [genshin.py](https://github.com/seriaati/genshin.py), and may need an update when HoYoverse changes something.
- Your characters, plan and inventory are in the database and its backups (a backup is taken before each HoYoLAB sync), but not yet in the snapshot export or the mirror folder.
- The app contains none of the game's images. It downloads each one from paimon.moe or Project Amber the first time a page shows it, then keeps it. To use the app offline, Settings → Images downloads them all at once.
