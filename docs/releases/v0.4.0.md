Genshin Companion 0.4.0 adds your weapons to your collection, counts them in the overview, and gives the pages more room.

## Download

| System | Archive |
|---|---|
| Windows (x86_64) | `genshin-companion-0.4.0-windows-x86_64.zip` |
| macOS 11 or later, Apple Silicon | `genshin-companion-0.4.0-macos-apple-silicon.zip` |
| macOS 11 or later, Intel | `genshin-companion-0.4.0-macos-intel.zip` |
| Linux (x86_64, glibc 2.35 or later) | `genshin-companion-0.4.0-linux-x86_64.tar.gz` |

Unpack the archive and start the app: there is nothing to install. Your data stays where it was, so a newer version picks up your history, plan and settings. `SHA256SUMS.txt` holds the archives' checksums.

The builds aren't signed by a developer account, so the first launch asks for confirmation:

- **Windows:** if SmartScreen warns, choose *More info*, then *Run anyway*.
- **macOS:** macOS refuses to open it the first time. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security and choose *Open Anyway*.
- **Linux:** the app needs fontconfig, freetype, libxkbcommon, Wayland, libX11, libxcb and the Vulkan loader, which desktop systems usually have.

## What's new

**Your weapons.** Archive → Weapons lists the weapons you have first, each card with its best refinement and how many copies. A weapon's page lists your copies, one row each: level, refinement, the character holding it, favourite. Add a copy with +, or remove one (the app asks first). A character holds one copy at a time, and its *My character* panel shows which. The weapons your characters hold come in with the HoYoLAB sync.

**The Weapons tab of the overview** now counts the weapons you own against the game's: by rarity and type, copies at R5 and copies held, then each of your 5★ and 4★ with its refinement, its copies and who holds them. A weapon opens its page. It used to count the weapons your wishes brought.

**The ascension planner:** *Add a weapon* starts from your most levelled copy that can still go up, as *Add a character* starts from your levels and talents.

**More room:**

- The wish history's rows are taller, with icons big enough to tell the items apart; the 5★ history of the banner view too.
- Settings is a centred column, or two on a wide window. Import and Credits are centred too.
- Page and section titles no longer have an icon before them.

## Good to know

- HoYoLAB doesn't show the weapons in your bag, only those your characters hold: add the others on the weapon's page. Nor does it say whether a character at a level cap (20, 40 … 80) ascended: such a character counts as not yet ascended.
- Your characters, weapons, plan and inventory are in the database and its backups (a backup is taken before each HoYoLAB sync), but not yet in the snapshot export or the mirror folder.
- The app contains none of the game's images. It downloads each one from paimon.moe or Project Amber the first time a page shows it, then keeps it. To use the app offline, Settings → Images downloads them all at once.
