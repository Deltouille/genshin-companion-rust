Genshin Companion 0.9.0 has a new look: quieter, in neutral greys with one accent colour, smaller controls and the Inter typeface. The overview's Characters tab gets charts and new figures, dialogs no longer act on a key pressed out of habit, and the app is about a fifth smaller.

Unpack the archive and start the app: there is nothing to install. Your data stays where it was, so a newer version picks up your history, plan and settings. `SHA256SUMS.txt` holds the archives' checksums.

The builds aren't signed by a developer account, so the first launch asks for confirmation:

- **Windows:** if SmartScreen warns, choose *More info*, then *Run anyway*.
- **macOS:** macOS refuses to open it the first time. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security and choose *Open Anyway*.
- **Linux:** the app needs fontconfig, freetype, libxkbcommon, Wayland, libX11, libxcb and the Vulkan loader, which desktop systems usually have.

## What's new

**A new look.** The app leaves the Genshin menus' slate and parchment for something simpler, in the light and dark themes alike:

- neutral greys, and one indigo accent for what is current or primary: the tab you're on, the main button, sliders, the focus ring;
- buttons, pickers, fields and filters at 32 px with small rounded corners instead of large pills, and a slimmer sidebar;
- the Inter typeface;
- colour kept for what the game gives it: rarities, elements, the item tiles and the charts.

Buttons without a fill no longer flash white when you click them.

**More about your characters.** *Overview → Characters* now shows:

- how your collection grew: the characters released by each version, and how many of them you own;
- your 5★ and 4★ constellations, your characters' levels and your talent levels as charts, with the average level of each talent;
- what raising your characters to where they are has cost: Mora, Hero's Wit, talent books, weekly boss materials, Crowns of Insight, and the resin all that takes on average;
- your characters by region, beside the counts by element and weapon.

**Dialogs that keep your data.**

- In a dialog that removes or replaces something (removing a plan entry, undoing an import, restoring a backup…), *Cancel* has the focus and Enter only closes it: only the button acts.
- A dialog you type in no longer closes when you click beside it, which lost what you had typed. Escape and *Cancel* still close it.
- A plan entry's grip opens a menu to move it (to the start, one place earlier or later, to the end), so the plan can be reordered with the keyboard too.

**A smaller app.** It is built with link-time optimisation: about a fifth smaller once unpacked.

**Sharper icons in the wish history.** The icons of the characters and weapons you wished for come from Project Amber now, like the rest of the app's images, so each image is downloaded once. The copies earlier versions took from paimon.moe are removed from the image cache.

**"Farm today" is gone** from the planner: it took a whole row for little.

**Fixes:**

- A database that claims a negative schema version is refused untouched, instead of opening without its migrations.
- A failure to read an imported file, or the image cache, is reported in your language.
- A character page whose talent description held a stray "{" could crash the app.
- Counts read back from the database are checked: a value out of range is an error, never another material or a 0.

## Good to know

- Your data and exports keep their formats: nothing in the database changes, and 0.8.1 still reads a snapshot exported by 0.9.0.
- The app contains none of the game's images. It downloads each one from Project Amber, and the banners' art from paimon.moe, the first time a page shows it, then keeps it. To use the app offline, Settings → Images downloads them all at once.
