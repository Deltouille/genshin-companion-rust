Genshin Companion 0.10.0 brings the Battle Pass into the planner: tell it the level you reached, and it counts the rewards still to come in what your plan needs.

## Download

| System | Archive |
|---|---|
| Windows (x86_64) | `genshin-companion-0.10.0-windows-x86_64.zip` |
| macOS 11 or later, Apple Silicon | `genshin-companion-0.10.0-macos-apple-silicon.zip` |
| macOS 11 or later, Intel | `genshin-companion-0.10.0-macos-intel.zip` |
| Linux (x86_64, glibc 2.35 or later) | `genshin-companion-0.10.0-linux-x86_64.tar.gz` |

Unpack the archive and start the app: there is nothing to install. Your data stays where it was, so a newer version picks up your history, plan and settings. `SHA256SUMS.txt` holds the archives' checksums.

The builds aren't signed by a developer account, so the first launch asks for confirmation:

- **Windows:** if SmartScreen warns, choose *More info*, then *Run anyway*.
- **macOS:** macOS refuses to open it the first time. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security and choose *Open Anyway*.
- **Linux:** the app needs fontconfig, freetype, libxkbcommon, Wayland, libX11, libxcb and the Vulkan loader, which desktop systems usually have.

## What's new

**The Battle Pass in the planner.** *Planner → Ascension* has a *Battle Pass…* button, which opens a panel at the side of the page:

- the level you reached, free or with the Gnostic Hymn, and the reward plan you picked in the game (three reward types of four);
- with *Count in “Still needed”* on, the Mora, EXP books and enhancement ores of the levels still to come count as if they were already in your bag;
- the boxes whose content you pick (Guides, Philosophies, local specialties) go to the series you choose for each, or, left to the planner, to what your plan lacks, in its order;
- the panel lists what the levels still to come give, and what each kind of box becomes;
- Fragile Resin isn't counted: you may well keep it for later;
- a new pass starts every 42 days, and the level then goes back to 0 by itself; *Reset to 0* does it by hand.

Each account has its own pass. The rewards come from the Genshin Impact Wiki: they have been the same in every version so far.

**Smaller changes:**

- The “Still needed” card loses its line of explanation.
- The archive's filters and the Battle Pass open in the same side panel, which no longer slides when your system asks for reduced motion.

**Fixes:**

- The `url` library is updated to 2.5.9 for a security advisory (RUSTSEC-2026-0592): an address nested thousands of times could overflow the stack.

## Good to know

- Your database gains the Battle Pass's table. The app takes a backup before changing it, and once 0.10.0 has opened your data, 0.9.0 refuses to open it rather than risk it.
- The Battle Pass isn't in snapshots or the mirror folder, since it lasts one version: the snapshot format doesn't change, and 0.9.0 still reads a snapshot exported by 0.10.0.
- The app contains none of the game's images. It downloads each one from Project Amber, and the banners' art from paimon.moe, the first time a page shows it, then keeps it. To use the app offline, Settings → Images downloads them all at once.
