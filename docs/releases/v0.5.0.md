Genshin Companion 0.5.0 tells you, in the ascension planner, how much resin and how many days farming what you still need takes.

## Download

| System | Archive |
|---|---|
| Windows (x86_64) | `genshin-companion-0.5.0-windows-x86_64.zip` |
| macOS 11 or later, Apple Silicon | `genshin-companion-0.5.0-macos-apple-silicon.zip` |
| macOS 11 or later, Intel | `genshin-companion-0.5.0-macos-intel.zip` |
| Linux (x86_64, glibc 2.35 or later) | `genshin-companion-0.5.0-linux-x86_64.tar.gz` |

Unpack the archive and start the app: there is nothing to install. Your data stays where it was, so a newer version picks up your history, plan and settings. `SHA256SUMS.txt` holds the archives' checksums.

The builds aren't signed by a developer account, so the first launch asks for confirmation:

- **Windows:** if SmartScreen warns, choose *More info*, then *Run anyway*.
- **macOS:** macOS refuses to open it the first time. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security and choose *Open Anyway*.
- **Linux:** the app needs fontconfig, freetype, libxkbcommon, Wayland, libX11, libxcb and the Vulkan loader, which desktop systems usually have.

## What's new

**Resin and days to farm, as on genshin-center.** The planner works out how much Original Resin farming what you still lack takes on average, once your inventory is shared out:

- Each card shows its resin and days; hover it for the runs of each source.
- *Still needed* shows the whole plan's: the total, the days, then the runs and resin of each source.
- Talent books and weapon materials count from level IV domains (20 resin a run): lower tiers craft up three for one, so a run is worth what it gives of every tier still needed.
- Boss materials count per boss (40 resin a run), Hero's Wit and Mora from ley lines (20 resin a run).
- *Resin options…* sets your world level (8 by default), the resin you spend a day (180 regenerate) and which sources to count.

For a character from level 1 to 90 with every talent to 10, at world level 8: 121 runs of talent books, 18 boss runs, 69 of EXP and 118 of Mora, about 6,880 resin or 38 days.

## Good to know

- The averages are those the Genshin Impact wiki publishes, gathered by the Genshin Data Gathering team. They are averages: your runs will give more or less.
- Weekly bosses, elemental gems, weapon ores, enemy drops and local specialties aren't counted, as on genshin-center. Nor are the days each domain opens.
- Your characters, weapons, plan and inventory are in the database and its backups, but not yet in the snapshot export or the mirror folder.
- The app contains none of the game's images. It downloads each one from paimon.moe or Project Amber the first time a page shows it, then keeps it. To use the app offline, Settings → Images downloads them all at once.
